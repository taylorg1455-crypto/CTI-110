# Teach a robot to make a PB & J sandwich

## Assumptions
- We have a fully charged human robot
- There is a countertop with the following items:
    - butter knife
    - jar of PB
    - jar of jelly
    - Two slices of bread laid side by side on a plate
## Steps
1. Position in front of items
2. Open lid of PB by turning the lid
3. Open lid of jelly by turning the lid
4. Grab butter knife
5. Scoop PB out of jar with butter knife
6. Spread PB over the large flat surface of one of the bread slices
7. Scoop some jelly with the butter knife
8. Spread jelly over the large flat surface of the other slice of bread
9. Put down the knife
10. Pick up PB Bread from underneath
11. Place the slice of PB on the slice of jelly with the PB and Jelly touching each other
12. Put lid back on the PB
13. Put the lid back on the jelly
14. Put jars and knife away

## Done When
- Sandwich is constructed
- Station must be clear except for the plate and sandwich
